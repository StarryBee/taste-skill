As the evening primroses began their nightly unfurling and the last rays of the amber sun dipped below the Heatherwick Hills, a sight of breathtaking wonder unfolded across the skies of the Enchanted Realm. Planting by the Moon: A Complete Guide for the Enchanted Garden

Lunar Planting Calendar — May 2026

The practice of planting by the moon is as old as the stars themselves. Our ancestors understood that the gravitational pull of the moon affects moisture in soil, just as it moves the great ocean tides. During the waxing moon, sap rises; during the waning moon, it retreats to the roots.

Plant above-ground crops during the waxing moon, root vegetables during the waning moon. Harvest during the full moon when plants are fullest of their vital essences. Leave the dark moon for rest, planning, and soil preparation.

The Mushroom Oracle of Pinewick Glen

Deep in Pinewick Glen, where the pines stand so tall they seem to hold up the sky itself, there grows a ring of ancient mushrooms. Not just any mushrooms — these are the Speaking Mushrooms, the ones the old folk called the Mycelium Council, each one a pale luminous ivory with caps so wide a dormouse might use one as a parasol.

They say that once in a generation, a child pure of heart may enter the ring at midnight under a waning crescent moon, and the mushrooms will speak. They will answer three questions — no more, no less — and the answers, while always true, are never what you expect.

Young Clover Ashwick was eleven years old when she learned of the mushrooms from her grandmother. The family had never worked up the courage to try. But Clover was different — she had her grandmother's green thumb and her grandfather's stubborn heart…

Beverages

Moonflower Elderflower Cordial

⏱ 45 min🍽 Makes 2L✦ Easy
Ingredients
  • 25 elderflower heads, freshly gathered at dawn
  • 1.5 kg unrefined cane sugar
  • 1.5L spring water
  • 2 unwaxed lemons, sliced
  • 1 tsp citric acid · a handful of fresh mint
Method

Bring water and sugar to a gentle simmer until dissolved. Add elderflower heads, lemon, mint, and citric acid. Cover and steep overnight in a cool, moonlit space. Strain through muslin into sterilised bottles. Dilute 1:5 with sparkling water.

✦ Most potent when stirred sunrise-clockwise and bottled under a waxing moon.

Lavender Honey & Rose Petal Cake

⏱ 1hr 20min🍽 Serves 8✦✦ Intermediate
Ingredients
  • 225g salted butter, room temperature
  • 200g golden caster sugar · 3 tbsp lavender honey
  • 4 free-range eggs · 225g self-raising flour
  • 2 tsp dried culinary lavender · zest of 1 lemon
  • Frosting: cream cheese, icing sugar, rose water, crystallised rose petals
Method

Beat butter, sugar and honey until pale and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time. Fold in sifted flour, lavender and lemon zest. Bake at 180°C for 25 minutes. Beat cream cheese with icing sugar and rose water until silky. Sandwich layers and decorate with crystallised petals.

✦ Best enjoyed in the garden with chamomile tea while the afternoon light turns to gold.

Hearty Supper

Wild Mushroom & Thyme Cottage Pie

⏱ 1hr 30min🍽 Serves 4✦✦ Intermediate
Ingredients
  • 500g mixed wild mushrooms (chanterelle, porcini, oyster)
  • 2 shallots · 4 garlic cloves · 200ml dark ale
  • 1 tbsp thyme leaves · 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 800g floury potatoes for mash · truffle oil
Method

Sauté shallots, add garlic and mushrooms until golden. Pour in ale and soy sauce; simmer to a thick gravy. Season with thyme. Crown with creamy mash and bake at 200°C until golden and bubbling at the edges.

✦ The mushrooms whisper of the forest floor. Listen while you cook.

Preserves

Rose Hip & Hawthorn Jelly

⏱ 2hr🍽 Makes 4 jars✦ Easy
Ingredients
  • 500g ripe rose hips (gathered after first frost)
  • 300g hawthorn berries · 600ml water
  • Juice of 1 lemon · preserving sugar
Method

Simmer hips and haws for 40 minutes until soft. Strain through a muslin jelly bag overnight. Add equal weight of sugar to the juice. Bring to a rolling boil, test for set, then jar while hot.

✦ A jewel-red jelly, rich in wild Vitamin C. A spoonful wards off winter and sadness both.

Candle Making

Beeswax Moon Candles with Pressed Wildflowers

✦ Beginner Friendly
You will need:
  • Raw beeswax pellets (500g)
  • Cotton wicks · Moon-shaped silicone moulds
  • Pressed wildflowers (violets, ferns, chamomile)
  • Lavender or rose essential oil
1.Melt beeswax in a double boiler. Add essential oil.
2.Pour a thin layer into moulds. Allow to semi-set.
3.Press wildflowers gently onto the surface. Pour remaining wax over.
4.Insert wick and cool completely — at least 4 hours — before unmoulding.

Embroidery

Botanical Embroidery: Stitching Living Patterns

✦✦ Intermediate
You will need:
  • Natural linen fabric, hooped tightly
  • Stranded cotton in sage, dusty rose, cream, forest green
  • Embroidery needle (sizes 7-9)
  • Botanical template or freehand sketch
1.Transfer your design with an air-erasable pen.
2.Begin with stem stitch for all branches and tendrils.
3.Fill leaves with satin stitch, following the natural direction.
4.Use French knots for berry clusters and flower centres.

Dried Flower & Herb Cottage Wreaths
